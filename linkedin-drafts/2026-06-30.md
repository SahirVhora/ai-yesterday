# LinkedIn Post Draft - 2026-06-30

Enterprise AI is not stuck because the models are weak.

It is stuck because most companies still have no clean path from "impressive demo" to "safe workflow inside a real system."

The latest AI Yesterday briefing included HP expanding its OpenAI Frontier partnership across customer experience, software development, and enterprise operations.

That is the part I find interesting. Not the announcement itself - the pattern.

In my SAP SuccessFactors work, the useful AI tools are rarely generic chatbots. They are narrow tools that sit close to the messy work:

- scanning configuration debt
- checking role-based permissions
- validating migration files
- exposing system metadata through MCP servers
- helping consultants ask better questions before a cutover

The business value is not "AI everywhere." It is fewer blind spots, faster audits, and less manual rework in systems where mistakes are expensive.

That is why enterprise AI will be won by teams who understand both the workflow and the controls around it.

My takeaway: the next wave of AI adoption will look less like magic and more like practical plumbing.
